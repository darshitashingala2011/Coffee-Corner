Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon after roasting are your whole beans shipped?

We roast in small batches 4 mornings each week. Online orders are packaged within 24 hours of roasting and shipped in one-way degassing valve bags to guarantee peak freshness.

Do you cater private office events, weddings, or cuppings?

Yes! We operate a bespoke mobile espresso bar complete with commercial dual-boiler machines and certified baristas for up to 300 guests. Contact us via the form above for bookings.

What milk alternatives do you offer for espresso drinks?

We stock barista-grade Oatly Oat Milk, Organic Almond Milk, Coconut Milk, and Soy Milk. All steam smoothly into velvety microfoam ideal for latte art.

How does the Coffee Corner Bean Rewards Program work?

Every dollar spent earns you 10 Bean points. When you register, you receive 100 bonus beans! Beans can be redeemed in your dashboard for free specialty drinks, pastries, or whole-bean bags.
